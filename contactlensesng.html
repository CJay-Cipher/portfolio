Terms & Conditions

1. Products & Prescriptions

Contact lenses and prescription eyeglasses require a valid prescription from a licensed optician. ContactLensesNG is not responsible for incorrect prescriptions provided by customers.

2. Payments

We accept payments via Paystack (card, bank transfer, USSD) and PayPal. All transactions are encrypted and secure. Prices are listed in Nigerian Naira (₦).

Unopened products may be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Contact lenses that have been opened cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.
